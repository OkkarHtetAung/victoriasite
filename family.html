Your Family Story

the people who made you, you

Apar

always caring, always nurturing

When dengue hit you at 8, it was traumatic. But it was Apar who stayed beside you through it all — the softest heart, the kindest hands. He took care of you when you needed it most. That experience changed you, made you stronger. And it was with him, in those quiet days of recovery, that you started eating meat.

The one who is always caring, always nurturing. Soft, kind, and there — no matter what.

👩

Amoe

the strict, reliable, strong woman

Amoe was different. She was strict. She gave you every kind of punishment for being too playful. She always pushed you and your sister to grow into women who could stand on their own — women who would never have to depend on anyone for their life.

She is the reason you are independent, responsible, and strong today. Strict, yes — but reliable, wise, and always right when it mattered.
