Your Leadership Story

from follower to leader

The Beginning

Where It All Started

The first time you started coming to church, meeting new people, making new friends — it was simple. You were just a girl in a room full of strangers who slowly became family.

No one knew what was coming. Not you, not them. But that simple beginning was the seed of everything.

2021

The Revolution

Then 2021 struck. You witnessed what it takes to stand with the right — the cost, the weight, the loneliness of it.

And in that moment you made a quiet decision that changed everything: you chose never to go back to the feared self again.

Same Year

Becoming the Music Leader

As older people moved to foreign countries, the gap had to be filled. You and the other younger ones had to split the responsibilities — and you became the music leader.

You did not know what to do. You did not know how to present, what responsibilities to take. But slowly, with time, you learnt everything.

Standing Firm

The Arguments, The Team

Time passed. You interacted with the older people of the church many times. You argued about right standing with right. You were challenged, questioned, pushed.

But you always stood with your team. Always.

2024

From Music Leader to Youth Leader

You became, from music leader, to the whole youth team leader. Your responsibilities rose.

And with rising responsibilities, you had to get stronger. Become better. Improve yourself. Always keep improving.

The Cost

When Love Turned to Whispers

Many times, the same people who used to love you, who used to take care of you, started looking at you as an arrogant person — because you stood with yourself, and it did not align with their view.

They blamed you. They talked behind your back. But you chose to ignore it. You chose to do what was required to be done.

Last April

Pyin Oo Lwin

With experience came vision. You led the team to the Pyin Oo Lwin trip last April. It was a great first-time experience for everyone — and for you, it was unforgettable.

You saw your team grow. You saw yourself grow. That trip was proof of everything you had built.

You Now

Stronger, Better, Still Standing

You get stronger. You get better. People start looking at you differently — with respect now, not doubt.

And there are also people who stayed. No matter what happened. No matter what was said. They stayed. Those are the ones that matter.

Leaders are made, not born. 💛
