Your Musical Journey

where the music began

🎶 Music From the Womb

Before you could speak, before you could even see, you already knew music. Whenever Amoe played songs while you were still growing inside her, you would move and settle — calm, listening. You always felt happier when Amoe played for you. And from that very first note, music became part of who you are.

🎻 Violin, From 3rd Grade

In the 3rd grade, you first held a violin. Small hands, big instrument, and a sound that took a while to find. But you kept at it — practising, learning, and completing your violin classes at an early young age. Something inside you already knew this was special.

🎹 Finding Your Passion After 2021

Then 2021 came. You started learning piano. You started singing. And somewhere between those first few chords and those first few songs, you understood — this is your passion. So you decided to follow it, no matter where it led.

🎤 Your First Experience

Going to a musical festival — the lights, the sound, the energy of people who love music the way you do. You saw your future there, and it was singing back at you.

👧 Teaching Youngsters in Hmawbi

You went to Hmawbi and taught other youngsters how to play the violin. Little hands, little bows, the same patience someone once gave you. You passed the music on. That is what makes it truly yours.
